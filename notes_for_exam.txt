Graph Analysis

1) Graph density
	- how many edges compared to how many it could have
	- ro(G) = number of edges(e) / number of edges of complete graph
	
	- for directed: ro(G) = e / (v * (v-1))
	- for undirected: ro(G) = 2 * e / (v * (v-1))

 	- dense graph: ro(G) -> 1
	- sparse graph: ro(G) -> 0

2) Types of graphs:
	- Small world graph
		average length: sum(distance(v1,v2)) for v1, v2 in graph.get_vertices() / v(v-1)
		average length < log (v)
	- Scale-free graphs
 		there are few vertices of very high degree, but on average the degree of vertices is low
		the vertices with high degree are called hubs

3) Analyze Centrality ~ how important a vertex or an edge is
	- degree centrality
		- difference between degree and average degree: centrality(v) = degree(v) / average_degree
		- hubs in scale-free graphs have a high degree centrality
		
	- betweenness centrality
		- edge betweenness centrality ~ how many shortest paths between 2 vertices have passed through this edge
		- betweenness centrality works with or without weights

		- the same for vertices ~ how many shortest paths go through a specific vertex

4) Clusters
	cluster ~ a subgraph that has much bigger density than the graph itself

	clusters with density 1? Cliques

	Finding biggest clusters with density > 0.8 ? (for example)
		application: finding language families
